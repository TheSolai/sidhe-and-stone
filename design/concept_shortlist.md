# GameStewKit — Ten Concept Shortlist (for Amre to choose)

All ten share the **same economy**: fairies hired into roles, each with two stats
that multiply into the relevant production step. All ten share the **Fate Reel**:
a bounded luck amplifier, identical maths underneath, wearing a different hat.

**The Fate Reel, once, for all ten.** The reel is a *marginal amplifier*, never
the foundation of the economy. It spins before a production step and modifies
that step's output:

| Outcome | Payout |
|---|---|
| Best | **2.6×** |
| Miss (neutral) | **1.0×** |
| Worst | **0.85×** |
| Luck effect | EV(0.60 luck) = 1.084 vs EV(0) = 1.031 |

Hard constraint: reel-attributable revenue must stay **under 45%** of gross.
Measured at **7.8%** in the 365-day harness. If a concept's reel ever needs to
carry the business, it is the wrong concept.

Each concept names its reel differently, but the payout table is identical —
so balance work done on one transfers to all ten.

---

## 1. The Bracken Brewery
*Ferment bog plants into spirits for a subterranean tavern.*

- **Loop:** harvest bracken & honey → ferment in casks (takes days) → serve
  patrons for coins and stories → unlock yeast strains and taps.
- **Fairies:** Honey-Drone (flavour), Smoke-Caster (ageing), Cask-Tender
  (stops spoilage).
- **Reel:** the **Clay Whiskey Wheel** — spin before sealing a cask.
- **Tension:** cheap bulk now vs. rare seasonal herbs for tiny, priceless batches.
- **Why cozy:** slow timers, warm cellars, a room filling with regulars.

## 2. The Harvest Mound
*Cultivate a sacred fairy circle of magical crops.*

- **Loop:** clear bracken → plant with farming fairies → tend through seasonal
  events → trade harvests for fertility → unlock crop tiers.
- **Fairies:** Bramble-Digger (weeds), Dew-Collector (hydration),
  Root-Whisperer (prevents blight).
- **Reel:** the **Seed Spinner** — a spinning top; shamrock landing triples a crop.
- **Tension:** sell now for cash, or leave plots fallow so fairies work overnight.
- **Why cozy:** grid planting, seasons, a patch that visibly improves.

## 3. Sidhe & Stone
*Excavate fairy mounds and ring-forts; curate the finds for scholars and donors.*

- **Loop:** excavate → catalogue → curate exhibits → attract scholars and donors.
  Static extraction, not biological growth — the opposite of farming.
- **Fairies:** Gravedigger (dig speed), Archivist (catalogue quality).
  Stat: **Curiosity**.
- **Reel:** the **Lucky Penny Dig** — a coin in the spoil; doubles a find's worth.
- **Tension:** vault it (safe, quiet income) or display it (more income, theft risk).
- **Why cozy:** filling a collection, tidy strata of pixel dirt, a quiet museum.

## 4. Harps & Halls
*Record traditional folk music from bards and spirits; release and tour it.*

- **Loop:** book studio time → record tracks → master albums → schedule tours.
  Audio production, not liquid consumables.
- **Fairies:** Session Player (track quality), Sound Tech (recording speed).
  Stat: **Inspiration**.
- **Reel:** the **Ghost Writer's Note** — a track finishes itself, free.
- **Tension:** invest in studio acoustics (track value) or stay lean (noise risk).
- **Why cozy:** sound waves as pixel art, instruments, artists co-creating.

## 5. Lughnasa Lodge
*Organise the seasonal festivals to bring tourism and goodwill to the village.*

- **Loop:** design layout → hire stall keepers and vendors → manage crowd flow →
  collect revenue. Spatial flow, not item movement.
- **Fairies:** Stall Keeper (sales efficiency), Barker (crowd attraction).
  Stat: **Charisma**.
- **Reel:** the **Pooka's Prank** — a random festival event: bonus takings, or a
  drop in attendance.
- **Tension:** price per head high (margin) vs. low (footfall and reputation).
- **Why cozy:** stalls, decorations changing with the seasons, a town that grows.

## 6. The Pooka's Parcel Post
*Courier service across the Irish landscape, by secret paths.*

- **Loop:** accept deliveries → sort and pack → navigate routes via luck checks →
  tips and new maps.
- **Fairies:** Badger-Runner (shortcuts), Fog-Walker (weather), Shape-Shifter
  (impossible locations).
- **Reel:** the **Coin of the Road** — silver is a windfall fee; brass is a
  costly detour that yields rare cargo.
- **Tension:** safe long route vs. risky shortcut that damages parcels.
- **Why cozy:** tidy logistics puzzles, five-minute sessions.

## 7. The Banshee's Boarding House
*A sanctuary where lost souls find rest, tended by fairies.*

- **Loop:** collect spectral echoes → compose lullabies to soothe → release
  purified spirits for karma → unlock haunted rooms.
- **Fairies:** Weeper (soothes), Screamer (calms the restless), Harper
  (translates regrets).
- **Reel:** **Rune Stones** drawn from a pouch — matching runes trigger a
  minigame that clears a complex case instantly.
- **Tension:** quick turnover of minor spirits vs. long care for one complex
  spirit that unlocks a rare upgrade.
- **Why cozy:** gentle emotional labour, no violence, a house that feels lived in.

## 8. The Riddle of the Well
*Fairy oracles trade riddles and secrets for wisdom.*

- **Loop:** pose riddles to visitors → solve with fairy insight → earn Wisdom →
  exchange for map fragments → unlock areas.
- **Fairies:** Riddle-Master (harder puzzles), Memory-Keeper (no lost answers),
  Truth-Seeker (hidden clues).
- **Reel:** the **Drop-a-Coin** — coins into the well summon a riddle modifier;
  a star makes it trivially easy for a big payout.
- **Tension:** safe simple riddles for steady wisdom vs. hard ancient ones that
  can fail and anger visitors.
- **Why cozy:** quiet, unhurried, logic-puzzle satisfaction.

## 9. The Gae Bolg Keep
*A vertical tower on a fairy mound, defended against the night.*

- **Loop:** build room levels → defend waves with warrior fairies → loot artefacts
  → build higher floors and unlock lore.
- **Fairies:** Spear-Hurler (range), Shield-Bearer (tank), Trickster (confusion).
- **Reel:** the **Wheel of Spears** — a triple spear launches a lane-clearing
  ultimate.
- **Tension:** upgrade floors vs. keep fairies in the line; one breach is total.
- **Why cozy:** single-column portrait layout, one thumb, tidy base building.

## 10. The Bardic Circle
*A travelling troupe collecting folklore and awakening magic from sleeping stones.*

- **Loop:** gather folklore fragments → weave verses with musical fairies →
  perform at stone circles → unlock dialects and legendary songs.
- **Fairies:** Story-Weaver (lore depth), Tambourine-Shaker (crowd energy),
  Mnemonic-Keeper (prevents memory loss).
- **Reel:** the **Folklore Loom** — three threads; a perfect weave doubles
  reputation for the day.
- **Tension:** a familiar ballad for safe rewards vs. a new epic that may fail
  and silence the crowd.
- **Why cozy:** creative expression, personal repertoire, travel.

---

### Cut, and why

- **The Wild Hunt Riders** — combat. The strongest arcade hook of the twelve,
  but it breaks the cozy register and drags in stamina and equipment upkeep
  that the Fate Reel then has to babysit. Strongest concept if the genre
  target ever shifts from cozy to action.
- **The Lughnasadh Matchmaker** — swipe-to-match is genuinely native to mobile
  and the concept is sharp, but the tone leans into dating-simulation
  territory that reads wrong for a management game about maintaining the
  natural world. Also structurally thin: three screens, little to manage.

### My recommendation

**Sidhe & Stone** if we want the most distinctive game, and **The Bracken
Brewery** if we want the safest, most proven KairoSoft loop.

Sidhe & Stone's loop — excavate, catalogue, curate, exhibit — has a vault-vs-display
risk decision every single day, and collection-completion is deeply satisfying
to watch. Nothing else on this list has that daily risk/reward shape without
leaning on timers or combat. Its art is also the easiest to make beautiful:
pixel strata, small artefacts, museum cases.

Bracken Brewery is the more conventional choice. Ferment timers and a
tap-room filling with regulars are proven, and the reel ("spin before sealing
a cask") is perfectly natural. If the goal is *ship a polished vertical slice
fastest*, this is the one.

If she says "you pick", go **Sidhe & Stone**.
